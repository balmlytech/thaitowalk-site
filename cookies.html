Cookie Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Cookies are small files a website can store in your browser. This page explains the very limited use of cookies on this site and how you stay in control.

A note for the owner: this reflects the site as built today — an optional Google map plus the Flipdish online-ordering system on the Order page. No analytics, no advertising. If you add analytics, a chat widget or social embeds later, this page (and the consent banner) must be updated to list those cookies.

How we use cookies

We don’t use cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking. Cookies are only set by two features, and only when you use them:

  • The embedded Google map in the “Find us” section — it stays blocked until you choose to load it. If you never load the map, no Google cookies are set.
  • The Flipdish online-ordering system on our Order page — Flipdish sets cookies that are necessary to run your basket, checkout and secure payment. These are only set when you use the ordering page.

We also store one small item in your browser to remember your cookie choice so we don’t ask again on every visit. This is strictly necessary and isn’t shared with anyone.

Your choices

  • The banner — on your first visit you can Accept or Reject the optional map cookies. Reject is the default if you do nothing: the map stays blocked.
  • Change your mind anytime — use the link in the footer of any page to reopen the banner.
  • “Load the map” button — if you’ve rejected cookies but want to see the map just once, you can load it directly from the “Find us” section.
  • Your browser — you can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.
